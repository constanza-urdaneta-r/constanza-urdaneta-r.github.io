Constanza Urdaneta Romano

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Marketing at the University of Texas at Austin, specializing in the Marketing Strategy track. My research examines how internal organizational dynamics shape marketing effectiveness and firm performance. Broadly, I study how leadership structure, workforce composition, and employee behavior influence marketing-related outcomes. My work leverages large-scale employee-level data and causal inference methods to uncover the strategic implications of organizational design.

Prior to joining the doctoral program, I gained experience in the art market, working at both an auction house and a gallery. I hold a BBA in Marketing from the University of Texas at Austin, with a minor in Arts Management and Administration.

Research

The Gender Pay Gap in Marketing

Examines gender-based compensation disparities within marketing roles, exploring how organizational structure and internal labor dynamics contribute to persistent wage gaps.

The Role of the Chief Communication Officer in Employee Turnover: An Internal Marketing Exploration

This research investigates how the presence and structure of customer-focused leadership influence employee turnover and marketing outcomes, highlighting internal marketing mechanisms as strategic mediators.
