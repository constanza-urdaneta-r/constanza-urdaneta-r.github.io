Constanza Urdaneta Romano

Constanza is a Ph.D. candidate in Marketing, Marketing Strategy track, at the McCombs School of Business, University of Texas at Austin.

Her research examines how firms manage and communicate value across multiple stakeholder markets. One stream of her work investigates internal organizational dynamics and their implications for marketing, including how leadership structure, workforce composition, and employee behavior shape marketing processes and outcomes. Her broader research interests include brand positioning in talent markets, branding and packaging, and nutrition and the voluntary disclosure of product information.

Methodologically, she is interested in econometric methods and causal inference, machine learning, large language models (LLMs), and text analysis, including topic modeling.

Beyond her academic research, Constanza maintains an active interest in the art market, with a focus on art as an asset class, provenance, and collection management. Prior to entering the doctoral program, she worked in the art market at both an auction house and a gallery.

She holds a BBA and an MS in Marketing from the University of Texas at Austin, with a minor in Arts Management and Administration.
